🪐 Orbit

A modern goals and tasks manager to plan, prioritize, and track your personal objectives — right from your phone.

🚀 [Try the Live] https://renzosantelli11-ship-it.github.io/Orbit/

🔗 ✨ Key Features

	•	🎯 Unlimited Goals & Tasks: Organize as many goals as you want, each with its own tasks, color, and progress ring.
	•	📊 Multiple Views: Dashboard, Goals, Kanban, Eisenhower Matrix, Calendar, Weekly Review, and Statistics — work the way that fits you.
	•	🔁 Recurring Tasks: Set tasks to repeat on chosen days of the week, just like a habit.
	•	🔒 Task Dependencies: Lock a task until another one is completed, so your workflow stays in order.
	•	🏷️ Cross-Goal Tags: Filter tasks by tags (Work, Health, Personal…) independent of which goal they belong to.
	•	📈 Metric Tracking: Track goals with a number instead of tasks (weight, savings, distance…) with a built-in trend chart.
	•	🧭 Eisenhower Matrix: Automatically sorts tasks into “Do now / Plan / Delegate / Rethink” based on urgency and priority.
	•	🗓️ Weekly Review: Compare planned vs. completed each week, with one-tap rescheduling for pending tasks.
	•	💡 Smart Insights: Automatic, client-side insights from your own history (most productive day, average tasks per week, and more).
	•	⭐ Milestones: Highlight key tasks so they stand out on the Dashboard and Calendar.
	•	🎮 XP & Levels: Earn XP for completed tasks and level up as you stay consistent.
	•	📋 Goal Templates: Start a new goal from a ready-made template (language, fitness, reading, finances, habits).
	•	🔒 Privacy First: Your data is stored safely and locally on your device.
	•	📱 Mobile-Optimized: Designed specifically to run smoothly on smartphones.
	•	🆓 100% Free: No ads, no paywalls, and no hidden subscriptions.
